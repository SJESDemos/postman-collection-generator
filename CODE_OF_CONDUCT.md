# Community Conduct

Project discussions and contributions must remain professional and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate with respect and technical specificity.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope, security, and release readiness.
- Report harmful or unsafe behavior privately when public discussion would increase harm.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, credentials, or misleading security claims
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer has set a clear boundary

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect
the project and its community. Report conduct concerns through the private channel documented in
SECURITY.md.
